# Helios Array: Independent Technical Audit, Q2 2027

Prepared by the Systems Assurance Office.

## 1. Scope

A six-week review of the Q1 2027 Engineering Report, covering measurement
methodology and cost projection. Two material findings follow.

## 2. Throughput Under Daytime Conditions

### 2.1 Replication

The Q1 report cites 780 GB/s aggregate downlink. We reproduced that figure and
confirm it under night-cycle conditions with the shroud deployed.

We also ran 22 daytime passes, which the Q1 report does not cover.

**Daytime sustained throughput: 415 GB/s.**

The 1.9x shortfall is attributed to thermal gradient across the primary bench
and consequent figure error, which rises to 61 nm RMS in direct illumination.

### 2.2 Assessment of the Q4 Target

The 1.2 TB/s target assumes night-cycle conditions throughout. Against a
daytime baseline of 415 GB/s it is not achievable by the stated date even with
the Ka-band refit. We recommend restating the target as 900 GB/s night-cycle
and publishing a separate daytime figure.

## 3. Cost Projection

The programme office projects final spend within the 210M allocation. Including
the amplifier refit and a schedule reserve for the transport overrun, we project
238-247M, a 13-18% overrun. This warrants board review before the August refit
is authorised.
