# Helios Array: Q1 2027 Engineering Report

## 1. Summary

The Helios Array reached first-light on its northern cluster in February 2027.
All twelve primary reflectors are installed and the control loop is closed.

## 2. Optical Performance

### 2.1 Alignment

Reflector alignment is held by the active support truss. Residual figure error
across the northern cluster measures 14 nm RMS.

### 2.2 Throughput

#### 2.2.1 Measurement Conditions

All throughput figures below were taken during night-cycle operation with the
thermal shroud deployed and ambient below -40 C. Daytime figures are not
included in this report.

#### 2.2.2 Recorded Throughput

Under night-cycle conditions the array sustains 780 GB/s aggregate downlink
across the four ground stations, averaged over 40 passes.

#### 2.2.3 Target

Engineering projects 1.2 TB/s by Q4 2027, contingent on the Ka-band amplifier
refit scheduled for August.

## 3. Thermal Management

The shroud holds the optical bench within 0.3 K of setpoint during eclipse.
Survival heaters have not been required since January.

## 4. Programme Cost

Spend to date is 61% of the 210M allocation. The largest single item was the
reflector transport contract at 24M, 9% above the original estimate.
